Imposter privacy notice

Imposter is a classroom learning game. This page explains what it stores when students and teachers sign in with Google.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name and school email address. We do not receive your Google password or access any other Google data.
  • Your class membership and the nickname your teacher saved for you.
  • Everything you type to the game's AI tutor, the tutor's replies, and your game progress.

Who can see it

  • Your teacher can read every chat you have in their class. Do not type anything you would not want your teacher to read.
  • If a message appears to involve self-harm, harm to others, or inappropriate language, your teacher is alerted by email with a link to the conversation.
  • Your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI so the tutor can reply. They are not used to show ads and are not sold.
  • Other students never see your email address or your chats.

Retention and your questions

Class records stay on the game's server until the teacher deletes the class or asks for them to be removed. To ask what is stored about you, or to have it removed, ask your teacher.
