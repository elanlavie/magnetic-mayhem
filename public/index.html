How a round works

A tag costs the crew one finished answer. Meetings start by themselves after a tag, then everyone votes. The crew wins by finishing the target share of tasks (ghosts count too) or by voting out every imposter. Imposters win when they equal the living crew, or when the optional timer runs out. About one imposter per five players. Needs at least 4 players.
